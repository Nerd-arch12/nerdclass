Reading Activities

Your Weekend Eating Schedule May Affect Your Heart

Research involving more than 100,000 adults in France examined “eating jetlag,” or differences between weekday and weekend meal times. Participants recorded what they consumed and their first and last eating times. Among men, every extra hour was linked to a 14% higher cardiovascular risk and a 21% higher coronary risk. The same association was not found in women.

Couples Share Musical Taste More than Personality

Three studies found that romantic partners tended to share more musical preferences than personality traits. Shared favorite musicians were linked to slightly greater tenderness, while shared musical dislikes were associated with slightly stronger feelings of love.

67% of Americans Change Their Personality at Work

A survey of US job seekers found that many adapted some aspect of their personality at work. Most considered adaptation important, although many questioned whether personality reflects job performance.

Advanced Grammar

Hedging

Use may, might, could, appears to and is associated with to express academic caution.

Irregular eating causes heart disease. → Irregular eating may be associated with greater risk.

Passive reporting

Use It is believed that... or subject + is believed to + verb.

Researchers believe music influences relationships. → Music is believed to influence relationships.

Inversion

After an initial negative expression, place the auxiliary before the subject.

People rarely question the cost. → Rarely do people question the cost.
